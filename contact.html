First visit

What to expect when you walk in

Step 01

Arrive ten minutes early

Or complete the intake form we email you and walk straight through to your room.

Step 02

Dress to move

Shorts or leggings, a t-shirt and running shoes cover nearly every assessment we do.

Step 03

Bring the paperwork

Insurance policy and certificate numbers, plus any imaging, surgical or specialist reports.

Step 04

Leave with a plan

You'll go home knowing the diagnosis, a realistic timeline and your first set of exercises.
