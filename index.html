Questions

The things people ask before booking

Still unsure? The 15-minute call is free and there's no pitch at the end of it.

Ask us directly
Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. Physiotherapy is a direct-access profession in Ontario, so you can book today. Some extended health plans still ask for a referral before they'll reimburse — worth a two-minute call to your insurer first.

Is physiotherapy covered by OHIP?

For most adults, no. OHIP-funded physiotherapy is limited to specific groups — generally people 65+, those under 19, and patients recently discharged from hospital, at designated clinics. We're a private clinic, so treatment is covered through extended health benefits, MVA insurance or WSIB.

Do you direct bill my insurance?

Yes, to more than twenty Canadian insurers including Sun Life, Manulife, Canada Life, Green Shield, Blue Cross and Desjardins. Bring your policy and certificate numbers to your first visit and we'll set it up while you're in the room.

I was in a car accident. What happens?

Bring your claim number and adjuster's contact. We complete the OCF-18 treatment plan, submit it through HCAI, and deal with the insurer directly. Under Ontario's accident benefits system your treatment is billed to the insurer, not to you.

What should I wear and bring?

Clothing you can move in — shorts or leggings and a t-shirt work for nearly every assessment. Bring your insurance details and any imaging or surgical reports. Arrive ten minutes early, or fill in your intake form online and walk straight through.

How many visits will I need?

Most people notice meaningful change within four to six visits. You'll get an honest estimate after your assessment and a formal re-test every four weeks, so you're never stuck in an open-ended plan.

Do you have parking, and how's transit?

Free surface parking on site. We're a five-minute drive from Cooksville GO, MiWay routes 19 and 26 stop at the door, and the clinic is ground-floor with step-free access from the lot.

What's your cancellation policy?

Twenty-four hours' notice, please. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged the full session fee, and insurers won't cover that one.
