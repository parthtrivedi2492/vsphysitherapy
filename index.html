Ask us
Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. Physiotherapy is direct-access in Ontario. Some insurance plans ask for a referral before reimbursing, so it's worth checking with your provider.

Is physiotherapy covered by OHIP?

For most adults, no. Treatment is covered through extended health benefits, MVA insurance or WSIB.

Do you direct bill?

Yes, to most major insurers. Bring your policy and certificate numbers to your first visit.

I was in a car accident. What now?

Bring your claim number and adjuster's details. We complete the OCF forms and submit through HCAI, and treatment is billed to your insurer.

What should I wear and bring?

Clothing you can move in, your insurance details, and any imaging or surgical reports.

Is there parking?

Yes — free on-site parking, with step-free access from the lot.

What's your cancellation policy?

Twenty-four hours' notice, please. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged the full fee.
