MVA & WSIB

Injured in a car accident or at work? We handle the forms so you can focus on recovering.

  1. 01

    Bring your claim number

    Open the claim with your insurer, then bring the number and your adjuster's details.

  2. 02

    We file the paperwork

    OCF forms submitted through HCAI, or a WSIB Program of Care — handled here.

  3. 03

    Treatment is billed to the insurer

    Approved treatment goes to your insurer, so you pay nothing at the desk.
