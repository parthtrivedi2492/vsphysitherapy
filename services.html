06

Claims

MVA & WSIB, handled end to end

Ontario's accident benefits system runs on forms. Sweni fills them in, submits them, and deals with the adjuster so you don't have to.

  1. MVA 01

    Bring your claim number

    Call your insurer to open the claim, then bring the claim number and adjuster's contact details to your first visit. That's the whole task list.

  2. MVA 02

    We complete the OCF-18

    Your assessment findings go straight into a treatment and assessment plan, submitted electronically through HCAI — the provincial claims system Ontario insurers use.

  3. MVA 03

    Treatment is billed to the insurer

    Under Ontario's Statutory Accident Benefits Schedule, approved treatment is billed to your auto insurer. You pay nothing at the desk.

  4. WSIB

    Workplace injuries

    We're an approved WSIB provider. Programs of Care, progress reporting and graduated return-to-work planning coordinated directly with your employer and case manager — common for warehouse, trades and airport-adjacent work around Malton.
