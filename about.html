A new clinic, built the way we'd want to be treated

VS Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Centre is a family-run practice on Explorer Drive, founded by Vivek and Sweni.

01

Our story

Why we opened

Too many clinics book three people into the same hour. We wanted to build the opposite: a calm space where an appointment belongs to one person, and where physiotherapy, chiropractic, massage, naturopathy and acupuncture actually talk to each other.

  • Brand-new private treatment rooms and equipment
  • Free on-site parking, step-free access
  • Evening and Saturday appointments
  • Direct billing, MVA and WSIB claims
02

What we believe

Three rules we don't bend

Listen first

Your history tells us more than any scan — and we explain what we find in plain language.

Measure, don't guess

Strength, range and function get tested and re-tested, so progress is visible on paper.

Discharge is the goal

When you don't need another visit, we'll tell you.

Your first visit

What to expect

  • Arrive ten minutes early, or fill in your form online
  • Wear something you can move in
  • Bring insurance details and any imaging reports
  • Leave with a diagnosis, a timeline and your first exercises
