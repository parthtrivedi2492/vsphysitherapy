Home — The Clinic

Two tables, one borrowed bike, 2013.

We opened in a small Cooksville unit with almost no equipment and one stubborn rule: nobody gets double-booked. Twelve years later that rule is still the reason people drive in from Milton.

01

Our story

What kept people coming back wasn't the equipment

The first year was two treatment tables, a borrowed exercise bike and a lot of word of mouth through the Cooksville and Erin Mills communities. People didn't return because we had the best machines. They returned because somebody actually sat down and listened to the whole story.

Today the clinic has a properly equipped rehab gym and private treatment rooms. What hasn't changed is the model: Sweni takes every appointment herself, so the schedule stays deliberately small.

  • Ground-floor and fully step-free, with free surface parking
  • Four enclosed private rooms — no curtain dividers anywhere
  • Rehab gym with turf lane, rack, sled and force testing
  • Five minutes from Cooksville GO; MiWay 19 and 26 at the door
02

What we believe

Three rules we don't bend

Listen before you touch

Your history tells us more than any MRI. We ask a lot of questions and then explain what we found in language that doesn't require a kinesiology degree.

Measure, don't guess

Range, strength and function get tested at baseline and re-tested every four weeks. Progress should be provable on paper, not just a feeling.

Discharge is the goal

We're trying to make ourselves unnecessary. When you don't need another visit, we'll say so — even though that's a strange business model.
